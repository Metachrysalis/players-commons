# Joining the Players Commons

## Who May Apply

Any person willing to accept the Players Commons Agreement may apply unless participation has been restricted under Commons governance.

## Application

Apply through the GitHub membership issue form or other process identified by the Commons.

An application should provide:

* preferred public name or pseudonym;
* GitHub identity or another verifiable identifier;
* confirmation that the applicant has read the License;
* confirmation that the applicant accepts the Commons Agreement; and
* any optional introduction the applicant wishes to provide.

Legal names need not be publicly listed merely for membership.

## Approval

Applications are handled according to `GOVERNANCE.md`.

Membership begins when the applicant is entered into `MEMBERS.md`, unless the approval record specifies another effective date.

## No Fee

Players Commons membership is not sold.

There is no required membership fee.
