# Players Commons Agreement

Membership in the Players Commons is a relationship of reciprocal participation.

## As a Player, I may:

* access Commons Material made available to Players;
* reproduce it;
* Share it under the Players Commons License;
* participate in Commons activities;
* seek membership in other Commons; and
* rely upon the Commons rights attached to material while I remain eligible.

## As a Player, I agree to:

* respect attribution;
* preserve licensing information;
* avoid falsely claiming authorship;
* avoid implying endorsement;
* respect other members and affected communities;
* comply with applicable law;
* avoid using membership to circumvent Organizational Use requirements; and
* act consistently with the integrity of the Commons.

## Membership Does Not Transfer Copyright

Joining the Players Commons does not transfer ownership of any work.

## Organizational Activity

Players acting materially on behalf of an organization must ensure that the organization possesses whatever Sustaining Organization authorization is required.

## Governance

Membership carries the participation rights defined in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

Financial contribution does not confer additional votes or status.

## Ending Membership

A member may leave.

The Commons may suspend or end membership according to the governance process.

Ending membership does not retroactively invalidate lawful past acts.
