# Metachrysalis Players Commons

The Players Commons is the broad participation layer of the Metachrysalis Commons.

Players may Share material placed in the Players Commons, subject to attribution and the other conditions of the Players Commons License.

Players do not automatically receive the right to Adapt Commons Material.

Creators Commons members receive Players Commons rights in addition to Creator rights.
